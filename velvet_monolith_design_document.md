# Design System: Velvet Monolith (SHAME.CO)

## Core Philosophy: Vibe-Coded Brutalism
Exposing hyper-designed, ecologically-bankrupt objects through a lens of high-fashion satire. The aesthetic is "Velvet Monolith"—heavy, expensive, and intentionally over-engineered.

## Typography
- **Primary Typeface:** `Space Grotesk`
- **Headings:** Bold, tight tracking (-0.05em), all-caps for impact.
- **Body:** Regular/Medium, clean sans-serif readability.
- **Wordmark:** "vibecodedsh*t" in plain, heavyweight letters (no emojis).

## Color Palette
- **Primary Surface:** `#CEA5C7` (Muted Velvet Pink)
- **Primary Text/UI:** `#471044` (Deep Plum Monolith)
- **Accents:** High-transparency variations of the primary plum for containers and buttons.

## Visual Language
- **Interactive Background:** Fixed full-viewport `<canvas id="spoon-canvas">` layer for depth and digital texture.
- **Corners:** High roundness for containers (Velvet feel) contrasting with sharp, brutalist typography.
- **Spacing:** Generous whitespace to simulate "luxury" and "exclusive" digital space.
- **Imagery:** Desaturated, high-contrast product renders with "REDACTED" overlays.

## Components
- **Top Navigation:** Sticky, high-blur backdrop, minimal links.
- **Hero:** Centered, massive typography, clear primary CTA.
- **Section Headers:** Small-caps labels (e.g., "PHASE 01") followed by large H2 titles.
- **Product Cards:** Softly rounded, tonal backgrounds, clear "Restorative Action" links.
