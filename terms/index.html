Methodology FAQ

Emergency Save terms

The emergency call

  • A paid call is a fixed slot both of us keep. You skip the line, and the call happens.
  • $100 for a 60-minute call, paid when you book. The fee is for the call, not the minutes.
  • You get a written diagnosis after the call: either one objective for a save day with a fixed quote, or a straight answer that it won't fit in a day, and why.
  • The $100 is separate from the save and is not credited toward it.
  • Non-refundable once booked. You can reschedule within the next 2 business days; after that the fee is kept. We try to accommodate last-minute changes.
  • Calls run on workdays, 09:00-18:00 Prague time. No weekend calls.

What we both commit to

A save day you book is kept for you. We do one full save per day, so that day is not offered to anyone else. In return you show up with access on time.

The objective

One written objective, agreed on the call and copied from the written diagnosis.

Price

Fixed, quoted on the call, minimum $1,000. It does not change if the work runs over.

Access

By the start of the save day you give us access to the repository, hosting and database needed for the objective. If access is not ready by then, the save day moves to the next open day. Late access moves the day and does not count as a missed objective.

Backup first

Before changing anything we take a backup of the code and database we were given access to, and tell you where it is.

If we don't finish

If the objective is not met by the end of the save day, we keep working on it at no extra charge until it is. That work comes before any new save we have not started.

If our day moves

If an earlier save runs over, your save day can move. We tell you as soon as we know, and you can then take the new day or cancel for a full refund of the save price.

Liability

Our total liability is capped at the save price you paid. We are not liable for indirect or consequential loss, including lost revenue or data, beyond restoring from the backup.

Your code stays yours

All work done on your codebase is yours. We keep no copy of your code or data after the save, apart from what you ask us to keep.

Confidentiality

We do not share your code, data or business details with anyone. Publishing a case study needs your written yes.

The agreement

Before the save starts you sign a one-page agreement with these terms, the objective and the price.
